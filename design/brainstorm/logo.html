PRISM logo brainstorm

Six concepts and the current wordmark. Each is shown at 160px, in the 48px site bar, as a 32px and 16px favicon, and on the dark desk. All marks are one colour (ink, using currentColor) except concept 3, which carries the highlighter yellow.

The risk line under each concept is a design-similarity scan against logos found online. It is not a legal trademark clearance.

Before adopting any mark, run a proper trademark search (CIPO in Canada, USPTO, WIPO Global Brand Database). The name itself needs checking: "PRISM" is already used in health literacy by the Program for Readability In Science & Medicine at Kaiser Permanente Washington Health Research Institute, and in health by Prism Health (Portland) and Prisma Health (South Carolina).
