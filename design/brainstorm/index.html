Before choosing

Name conflict: PRISM (Program for Readability In Science & Medicine) at Kaiser Permanente Washington is a plain-language health programme with the same audience. Prisma Health and Prism Health are nearby too.

The similarity check compares designs only and is not a trademark search. Search CIPO, USPTO and WIPO before adopting a mark or the name.
