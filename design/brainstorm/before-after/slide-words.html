Same handout, before and after.

As written

Your physician has prescribed furosemide, a diuretic, to reduce edema by increasing your urinary output. Administer one tablet orally each morning with food, monitor your weight daily, and contact the clinic immediately if you experience a weight gain exceeding 2 pounds within a 24-hour period, as this may indicate fluid retention. Approximately 25% of patients experience orthostatic hypotension during the initial weeks of treatment.

After PRISM

Your doctor gave you furosemide, a water pill. It brings down swelling by making you pee more. Take one pill by mouth each morning with food. Check your weight every day. Call the clinic right away if you gain more than 2 pounds in one day. This may mean your body is holding fluid. About 1 in 4 people feel dizzy when they stand up in the first weeks. Stand up slowly.
