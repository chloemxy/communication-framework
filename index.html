About PRISM

PRISM is a free, open-source tool that checks patient handouts, posters and videos against published frameworks, such as CDC’s Clear Communication Index and AHRQ’s PEMAT.

How it works

  1. 1Set the Brief. Who it’s for and what it is.
  2. 2Add your draft. Type, paste, or upload a file or image.
  3. 3Pick frameworks. The tool scores what it can measure. You rate the rest.

It grades your work; it doesn’t write it. Every check links to its source, and items you haven’t reviewed never count against you.

Why we built it

People who design patient materials often hear “this looks great” at handoff, and then nothing. We wanted a way to check a piece against established evidence, not opinion, before and after it goes out.
